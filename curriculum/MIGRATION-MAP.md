# Old-to-new mapping (skeleton)

Roadmap Stage 4 maps every existing teaching page to one of: **reuse**, **split**, **merge**, **rewrite**, **reference-only** or **retire**, with its destination IDs in the new curriculum. This skeleton lists the 103 live teaching chapters (595 teaching pages, counted from `js/data/learn/shelf.js` on 2026-11-27) with no decision yet. The 13 End-of-book Notes chapters (52 sections) are generated reference material and are not mapped. Decide at the chapter level first, then refine page by page. Never change a live URL until its mapping is reviewed and a redirect is tested (TECHNICAL-CHANGES.md, T3).

Decision values: `unmapped` (default), `reuse`, `split`, `merge`, `rewrite`, `reference-only`, `retire`.
