# Think Academy Year 2 materials: what was received and how they are used

**Status (2026-11-27).** The owner supplied all 22 lessons of Think Olympiad Year 2, Term A, as PDFs in this session: Lessons 1 and 2 as workbooks, Lessons 3 to 22 as annotated class slides. They are copyrighted. They are **not** in the repository and must never be committed, quoted, paraphrased or adapted. Their text, exercises, examples and figures are not reproduced anywhere in this curriculum (owner, 2026-11-27).

**How they are used.** Only as a general guide to the sequence of topics and the shape of a lesson, which the specification already summarises for the 22 anchor chapters. Every explanation, example and exercise in the new books is our own or comes from the contest archive.

**Reviewed so far, honestly stated.** All 22 files were identified from their title pages. The structure of Lesson 12 (power of a point) and the opening pages of Lesson 1 (sets and mappings) were looked at. No other lesson has been reviewed beyond its title page. A chapter's packet records which source lessons were consulted for structure; until that is recorded, treat the lesson as not reviewed. The owner can resupply a PDF when a chapter needs it (D10).

**Structural observations, in our own words.** A lesson recalls the earlier results it needs, develops each new idea through its cases with a proof, checks understanding with short direct questions, then works through problem sets that rise from computation to proof. The new standard (AUTHORING-STANDARD.md) keeps that progression and drops the source's generic section labels at the owner's request.
