# Technical changes for the new curriculum

This file records what the current Learn implementation assumes, and every change the twelve-book curriculum needs. None of it is implemented. Phase 2 of DEVELOPMENT-PLAN.md starts it, after the owner's decisions D1–D6 (REVIEW-NOTES.md §3). The guiding rule comes from spec/roadmap.md: preserve the reference cards, problem slugs, ratings and saved progress, and keep every live lesson URL working until a reviewed mapping and a tested redirect replace it.

## 0. How the current Learn works (inspected 2026-11-27)

| Piece | Where | What it assumes |
|---|---|---|
| Shelf | `js/data/learn/shelf.js`, generated by `tools/build-learn-shelf.py` from `LEARN-OUTLINE.md` | 13 books. Each chapter has a one-letter code plus a number (L1, M3, S12, A1, N9, G10, C10, P1, E1, D1, K1, O1). Lessons are `<chapter>.<n>`. Levels are Core, Core II or Extension; `part` headings; End-of-book Notes chapters have `kind: "reference"`. 116 chapters and 647 shelf pages: 103 teaching chapters with 595 teaching pages, plus 13 reference chapters with 52 sections. |

**The decisive finding.** The four live AIME books already use chapter codes A1–A10, G1–G10, N1–N9 and C1–C10. Their lessons are A1.1, G2.7, N3.4 and so on. The new curriculum uses the same strings for chapters: A1.1 means Algebra I, Chapter 1. 136 of the new chapter IDs fall into the same pattern, and most coincide exactly with live AIME lesson codes. Because content, summary, progress, topic selections and lesson links are all keyed by bare codes, the new IDs cannot enter the app unqualified (decision D1).

## T1. Identifiers (decision D1)

**Recommendation.** Keep the specification's IDs exactly as written: they are stable, and the spec, audits and pilots cite them. In the app and in every cross-system key, always qualify them by book: `<book-id>/<ID>`, for example `geometry-2/G2.7` for a chapter and `geometry-2/G2.7.4` for a concept section. Book IDs contain no dots and are unique across both shelves, so a qualified key can never collide with a live code. The live books keep their codes unchanged.

- Chapter-level data is keyed `MATH_CURRICULUM["geometry-2/G2.7"]`, a new global. This leaves `window.MATH_LEARN` untouched.
- Concept-section keys are `"G2.7.4"` inside that chapter object; cross-references outside it use the qualified form.
- Alternatives considered: (a) rename the live AIME codes, which is rejected because it breaks URLs, progress and lesson-links; (b) give the new IDs a letter prefix such as `V-A1.1`, which is rejected because it diverges from the specification and from every audit citation.

## T2. Data model and files (decision D2: who writes, and where)

- **Shelf.** Generate `js/data/curriculum/shelf.js` (`window.MATH_CURRICULUM_SHELF`) from `curriculum/data/index.json` with a new builder, `tools/curriculum/build-shelf.cjs`. Do not hand-edit it, and do not merge it into `shelf.js` until replacement.
- **Chapter files.** `js/data/curriculum/<book-id>/<id>.js`, for example `geometry-2/g2.07.js`, sets `window.MATH_CURRICULUM["geometry-2/G2.7"]`. Proposed shape: `opening` (the motivating exploration), `sections` keyed by concept ID, `review`, `transfer` and `delayed` problem sets, and `cards` (recap links shown after the idea, per T5).
  - Each section has `body`, `checks` (concept checks), `examples` (worked, with a recorded choice of method), `exercises`, `placements` (T6), `prereqIds` (curriculum IDs) and `bridges` (cross-subject IDs).
- **Optional material.** A `status: "optional"` flag at chapter level (A2.22) and section level (the sections the spec marks as extension or enrichment). Do not reuse Core, Core II or Extension (decision D5).
- **Ownership.** Codex owns `js/data/learn/**` and `tools/build-learn-shelf.py`. A separate `js/data/curriculum/**` avoids mixing the two shelves, but D2 must say who authors there.

## T3. Navigation and routes

- New routes: `#/learn/<book-id>/<id>` for a chapter (for example `#/learn/geometry-2/g2.7`), `/<n>` for concept section n, and `/review`, `/transfer` and `/delayed` for the problem sets. Book IDs are distinct from live ones, so `learnRoute` can dispatch on the book: a new-shelf book goes to a new `curriculumRoute` with its own pattern (`[a-z]\d\.\d+`). Existing routes do not change.
- **Visibility during development (decision D3).** Recommended: the new shelf is reachable only on a preview route, `#/learn/preview`, or behind a Settings toggle that is off by default. It is absent from the Learn landing page, the homepage, search and recommendations until a volume is released. This preserves "do not modify the live Learn experience".
- **On replacement**, one volume at a time: map each old lesson URL to its new destination (MIGRATION-MAP.md), add redirects in the existing `hashchange` redirect block (`js/app.js`, where `#/lists` and contest lists are already redirected), and keep the old chapter files loadable until the redirect tests pass.
- **Prerequisite navigation.** The cross-subject bridge table (spec/shelf-and-levels.md) becomes `bridges` links on chapter openings: "needs A2.16 (Trigonometric Identities I)". These are links, never gates, because the books are independently paced.

## T4. Shelf presentation

- Twelve covers in four subject rows and three volume columns, or a 4×3 grid. The volume label (Foundations, Intermediate, Advanced problem solving) sits on each cover. Our design language applies: no gradients except on covers, no sticky headers.
- Each chapter's contents page shows its basis label ("Year 2 anchor" or "Proposed") only in editorial or preview mode. The spec calls these source labels, not learner-facing structure ("do not expose taxonomy labels or metadata as the primary teaching structure").

## T5. Lesson rendering

The spec's lesson standard differs from CONVENTIONS §14 in ways the renderer must support. See decision D4.

1. **Opening before references.** The exploration comes first. Cards appear as optional recaps near the conclusion, never as the first block. Today §14 opens a lesson with the cards it teaches and assumes, so the new renderer must not put the card drawer at the top.
2. **A checkpoint before a revealed solution.** "Allow a learner to attempt a checkpoint before revealing a hint or worked solution" (spec/teaching-pilots.md). The existing check and exercise components already gate solutions behind an attempt. **Hints** conflict with the standing preference "never re-add … hints in the UI" (decision D4b).
3. **Faded guidance.** Worked example, then a partial example (missing its final step), then a close independent problem, then a mixed transfer task. This needs a "partial example" component: a worked solution with a final step the learner supplies.
4. **Variable structure.** "Do not force every lesson into identical headings." The renderer should accept free headings within a section body, which §14's single flowing explanation with no subheadings does not.
5. **Chapter structure.** The live X.1 Introduction and final Summary would shift every concept number by one (A1.1.1 would become lesson A1.1.2). Recommended: render the chapter's premise as an unnumbered opening page and the mastery outcome as an unnumbered close, so section n is concept n.
6. **Figures.** Geometry diagrams follow the existing figure rules (CONVENTIONS §4, the diagram and label checks).

## T6. Problem associations

- **Placement records.** The spec requires them for every exercise: `problem_slug`, `identity_group`, `route_id`, `chapter_id`, `required_concept_ids`, `teaching_role` (explore, guided, independent, transfer), `novelty`, `difficulty_source`, `review_status` (candidate, route-read, prerequisite-checked, published) and `review_note` (spec/coverage-audit.md, "Solution-level review"). Store them in `curriculum/data/placements/<book-id>.json` during authoring. Generate the app's per-chapter `problems` arrays from them, never by hand.
- **Identity groups.** Use `js/data/problems/problem-aliases.json`, the explicit canonical-ID map, so AMC 10/12 twins and the other 621 known duplicates count once.
- **Tracks (decision D6).** Today a problem is placed at most once per track (AMC; AIME). The spec places a problem at its teaching home and allows a separate transfer placement in Volume III. Recommended: the new shelf is one new track. Within it, a problem has one primary placement plus at most one Volume III transfer placement, and both records say which is which.
- **Read-only use of Codex's data.** The new curriculum reads the problem database, catalog, ratings and topic tree. It never writes them: they are Codex's scope, and the owner's instruction excludes them. Gaps found while placing problems go to QUESTIONS.md, as today.
- **Topic coverage.** The spec's 190 topic-node-to-chapter rules (spec/coverage-audit.md) were not supplied as data. Recreate them as `curriculum/data/topic-routes.json` (topic node ID → chapter ID), generated against the live tree. This replaces `lesson-topics.js` for the new shelf.

## T7. Cards

- A card links to new-shelf lessons only after a volume is released. Until then the live `MATH_LESSON_LINKS` stays the only source, so card pages are unchanged.
- **Teaching a card in several volumes.** The spec teaches a topic "in multiple volumes with explicitly different outcomes", while `check-learn.js` allows one teaching placement per card per track. Recommended rule for the new track: one *primary teaching* placement per card, plus any number of `revisit` links that carry the changed objective.

## T8. Search

- **Main reference search** indexes cards and lessons. New-shelf lessons stay out of it until release, then enter with qualified IDs. The semantic index (`search-vectors.*`) is rebuilt once at the end of a step, per the standing preference.
- **Book-scoped search** (`tests/book-search.cjs`; the Index and Glossary in the End-of-book Notes). Decide whether the new books get generated notes. Recommended yes: the builder already derives Index targets from chapter data. It would need a curriculum-shelf input and qualified targets, and these are Claude's files (`tools/build-book-notes.cjs` matching and indexing).
- Contest search and Practice are not affected.

## T9. Progress

- Use a separate store, `mq-learn2`, with `read`, `checks` and `problems`. Key checks `geometry-2/G2.7.4#n`. Add it to `PROGRESS_KEYS` so Export and Import carry it.
  - The alternative is reusing `mq-learn` with qualified keys. It is also safe, but mixes preview data into the live store.
- Problem progress stays keyed by slug and shared, since a solved archive problem is solved wherever it appears. Activity history (`learning-activity`) records new-shelf events under qualified IDs.
- **On replacement**, a mapping-driven migration copies read and checks state from old lesson codes to their new destinations. It applies only where the mapping is `reuse` or `rewrite` with an equivalent check, and it never invents progress.

## T11. Versioning and loading

New assets go into the inert on-demand template in `index.html` and carry `?v=` versions via `tools/bump-versions.py`. They only enter `index.html` in Phase 2. Until then nothing in this branch touches `index.html`.

## T12. Coexistence summary

During development the live 13 books, their codes, URLs, progress, search entries, card links and recommendations are untouched. The new shelf lives under new globals, files, routes and storage keys, reachable only in preview. Replacement happens volume by volume. Each volume needs its mapping reviewed, redirects and progress migration tested, the volume's chapters at S5 or better in TRACKER.md, and the owner's approval. It is followed by retirement of the corresponding old chapters' visibility, while their URLs keep redirecting.
