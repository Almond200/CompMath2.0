=== 1
You have $454$ grams of cheese. You give $1/4$ of the cheese to Bob and $1/3$ of the remainder to Alice. What is the difference (in grams) between the amount of cheese Bob got compared to the amount Alice got?
=== 2
Each side of ten coins is coloured either red, yellow, blue or green, so that each coin is painted two different colours. If three coins have the colour red, eight coins have the colour yellow, and five coins have the colour blue, how many coins have the colour green?
=== 3
Swiftie the snail decides to visit her friend Taylor in Edmonton. The distance between Swiftie's house in Calgary and Taylor's house is $288$ km. Allowing for rest breaks, Swiftie crawls at an average of $1$ cm per minute. How many days does it take Swiftie to make the journey?
=== 4
The points $A,B,C$ lie on a line, not necessarily in that order. The length $|AB|$ of the segment $AB$ is $15$ cm, and the ratio $|AC|:|BC|$ is $1:4$. What is the largest possible length in cm of the segment $AC$?
=== 5
The sum of five different prime numbers is $55$. What is the largest prime number that might be one of these five? Note that $1$ is not a prime number.
=== 6
Given a triangle $ABC$, there is a point $D$ on side $BC$ such that $AB$, $AD$ and $DC$ are of the same length. If $\angle BAD=76^\circ$, what is $\angle ACB$ (in degrees)?
=== 7
In the morning, Sarah sees a positive integer written on the blackboard. Every day, she erases it and writes the sum of its digits multiplied by $2$. For example, if $123$ is written on the board, she will write $12$, since $2\cdot(1+2+3)=12$; and if $3$ is on the board, then she will write $6$. If Sarah sees number $37$ on day $0$, what number will she see on day $2025$ before replacing it?
=== 8
Today is Penny's birthday! If you multiply Penny's age today by the age of her older sister Jooni, you get the age of their cousin Isaac. (Of course, all ages are in whole numbers of years.) The weird thing is, in a few years, when Penny becomes the age that Jooni is now, the sum of their ages at that time will be Isaac's age then! How old (in years) is Penny today?
=== 9
$ABC$ is a triangle, and points $D$ on the side $AB$, $E$ on the side $BC$ and $F$ on the side $AC$ are chosen in such a way that the ratios of the lengths are $|AD|:|AB|=|BE|:|BC|=|AF|:|AC|=3:5$. The area of the triangle $DEF$ is $18\text{cm}^2$. Find the area of the triangle $ABC$ in $\text{cm}^2$.
