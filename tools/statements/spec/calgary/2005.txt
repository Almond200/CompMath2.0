=== 2

=== 6
On each day that Adrian does his homework his mother gives him $\$4$, and on days he doesn't she takes $\$1$ away from him. After $30$ days Adrian notices that he has the same amount of money as when he started even though he has spent nothing and had no other source of income. On how many of the $30$ days did he do his homework?
