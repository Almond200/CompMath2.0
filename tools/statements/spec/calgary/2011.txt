=== 3
Doan mixes together $1$ litre of $1\%$ butterfat milk, $2$ litres of $2\%$ butterfat milk and $4$ litres of $4\%$ butterfat milk. What percentage of the resulting seven litres of milk is butterfat?
=== 4
Nine people, all with different heights, are sitting around a circular table. What is the greatest possible number of people that could be taller than both persons sitting next to him/her?
=== 5

=== 7
Sixteen coins, numbered $1$ to $16$, are each red on one side and blue on the other side. Initially, all coins have their red sides facing up. The coins that are multiples of $2$ are turned over. Then the coins that are multiples of $4$ are turned over. Then the coins that are multiples of $8$ are turned over. Then the coins that are multiples of $16$ are turned over. Afterwards, how many of the coins have the red side facing up?
