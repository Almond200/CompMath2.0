=== 1
The average of the four numbers $1,1,1$, and $N$ is $2$. Determine $N$.
=== 2
Alice has a box of candies. She gives $5$ candies to Bob, and then gives half of the remaining to Charolette. After that, Alice gives Daniel $1/3$ of the remaining, then she eats two candies and saves the remaining $4$ for later. How many candies did Alice have at the beginning?
=== 3
Dani has a box with $3$ red balls, $3$ green balls and $5$ blue balls. What is the minimum number of balls Dani has to pull out of the box (without looking at them) to guarantee she pulls at least one of each colour?
=== 4
Two points $A$ and $B$ divide a circle in the ratio $5{:}7$. What is the acute angle (in degrees) between the chord $AB$ and the tangent line to the circle at point $A$?

=== 5
A beetle named Jerry is looking for shoes to wear for his big day. Jerry has $2$ identical blue left-shoes, $1$ red left-shoe, $3$ identical blue right-shoes, and $1$ red right-shoe. In how many ways can Jerry put shoes on his six feet, three left and three right?
=== 6
Let $a,b,c$ be integers strictly greater than $1$ and strictly less than $100$ such that $a^3(b-c)(b+c)=2024$. What is the sum $a+b+c$?
=== 7
Each of the eight numbers $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8$ is either coloured red, or blue, or both red and blue. The sum of the numbers that have the colour red is $20$. The sum of the numbers that have the colour blue is $24$. What is the sum of the numbers that are coloured both red and blue?
=== 8
$ABC$ is an equilateral triangle with each side of length $4$ cm and a half-circle has $AC$ as a diameter. The half-circle intersects the legs $AB$ and $BC$ of the triangle at the points $D$ and $E$, respectively. The area of the trapezoid $ADEC$ can be written as $\sqrt N\ \text{cm}^2$, where $N$ is an integer. What is $N$?

=== 9
Notice that December $31$, $2023$ could be written in the form $12|31|23$, where the first pair of digits ($12$) indicates December (the $12$th month), the second pair ($31$) indicates the day of that month, and the third pair ($23$) are the last two digits of the year. What will be the next date that can be written in the form $ab|ca|bc$, where $a,b,c$ are digits? (Each day and month must be written as a two-digit number, if necessary by adding a zero at the beginning. For instance, May $1$, $2024$ would be written $05|01|24$.)
