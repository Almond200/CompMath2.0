=== 1
A rectangle with integer length and integer width has area $13\ \text{cm}^2$. What is the perimeter of the rectangle in cm?
=== 2
A nice fact about the current year is that $2016$ is equal to the sum $1+2+3+\cdots+63$ of the first $63$ positive integers. When Richard told this to his grandmother, she said: Interesting! I was born in a year which is also the sum of the first $X$ positive integers, where $X$ is some positive integer. In what year was Richard's grandmother born? (You may assume that Richard's grandmother is less than $100$ years old.)
=== 3
Suppose you reduce each of the following $64$ fractions to lowest terms:
$$\frac1{64},\frac2{64},\frac3{64},\ldots,\frac{64}{64}.$$
How many of the resulting $64$ reduced fractions have a denominator of $8$?
=== 4
Peppers come in four colours: green, red, yellow and orange. In how many ways can you make a bag of six peppers so that there is at least one of each colour?
=== 5
In the following addition of four-digit numbers, $X,Y$ and $Z$ stand for digits (not necessarily different). If the addition is correct, what digit does $Y$ stand for?

$$\begin{array}{rrrrr}&2&X&X&Y\\+&3&X&Y&Z\\\hline&Z&Y&X&X\end{array}$$

=== 7
A number was decreased by $20\%$, and the resulting number increased by $20\%$. What percentage of the original number is the final result?
=== 8
A group of grade $7$ students and grade $9$ students are at a banquet. The average height of the grade $9$ students is $180$ cm. The average height of the grade $7$ students is $160$ cm. If the average height of all students at the banquet is $168$ cm and there are $72$ grade $9$ students, how many grade $7$ students are there?
=== 9
If the straight-line distance from one corner of a cube to the opposite corner (i.e., the length of the long diagonal or body-diagonal of a cube) is $9$ cm, what is the area (in $\text{cm}^2$) of one of its faces?
