=== 1
What is the largest number of integers that can be chosen from $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9\}$ such that no two integers are consecutive?
=== 2
Elves and ogres live in the land of Pixie. The average height of the elves is $80\,\text{cm}$, the average height of the ogres is $200\,\text{cm}$ and the average height of the elves and the ogres together is $140\,\text{cm}$. There are $36$ elves that live in Pixie. How many ogres live in Pixie?

=== 4
What is the longest possible length (in cm) of a side of a triangle which has positive integer side lengths and perimeter $17\,\text{cm}$?
=== 5
$A$ and $B$ are whole numbers so that the ratio $A:B$ is equal to $2:3$. If you add $100$ to each of $A$ and $B$, the new ratio becomes equal to $3:4$. What is $A$?
=== 6
You are given a two-digit positive integer. If you reverse the digits of your number, the result is a number which is $20\%$ larger than your number. What is your number?

=== 8
Carol's job is to feed four elephants at the circus. She receives a bag of peanuts every day and feeds each elephant as many peanuts as she can so that each elephant receives the same number of peanuts. She then eats the remaining peanuts (if any) at the end of the day. On the first day Carol receives $200$ peanuts. On every day after, she receives one more peanut than she did the previous day. This was done over $30$ days. How many peanuts did Carol eat over the $30$ days?
