=== 1
The perimeter of a rectangle with integer edge-lengths is $10$cm. What is the largest area (in $\text{cm}^2$) that the rectangle can have?
=== 2
A store increases the price of a shirt by $10\%$, then reduces the cost by $\$10$. The price is then $90\%$ of the original price. Find the original price.
=== 3
At $1$PM Isaac had done $1/3$ of his homework. At $2$PM he had done $1/2$ his homework. He works at a constant rate all the time. At what time did he finish his homework?
=== 4
Melissa drives for $11$ minutes, the first minute at $10$ km/h, the second minute at $20$ km/h, and so on until the $11$th minute at $110$ km/h. What is the total distance (in km) she travelled?
=== 5
Shaan takes a $10$ cm by $10$ cm square piece of paper, and cuts circular holes of radius $1$ cm in it, not overlapping, so that more than half the area of the paper is removed. What is the smallest number of holes that he could cut?
=== 6
Let $A(0,0)$, $B(3,5)$, $C(3,0)$, $D(5,0)$ and $E(5,-5)$ be five points in the Cartesian plane. The pentagon $ABCDE$ and its reflection in the $x$-axis are combined to make a seven sided figure. What is the area of this figure?
=== 7
A lawn $10$ metres by $10$ metres receives $1$ cm of rain over its entire surface. Assuming that the volume of each raindrop is $1$ cubic millimetre, the number of raindrops that fell on the lawn can be written as a one followed by a number of zeros. How many zeros come after the $1$?
=== 8
The number $12$ has the strange property that the next number ($13$) is prime, the number after that ($14$) is twice a prime (since $14=2\times 7$) and the number after that ($15$) is three times a prime (since $15=3\times 5$). Find a number $N$ bigger than $12$ so that $N+1$ is prime, $N+2$ is twice a prime, and $N+3$ is three times a prime.
=== 9
Three positive integers $a,b,c$ are such that $0<a<b<c$ and $b-a$, $c-a$ and $c-b$ are all squares of integers. What is the smallest possible value of $c$?
