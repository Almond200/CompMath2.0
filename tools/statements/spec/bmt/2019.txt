=== algebra-1
How many integers $x$ satisfy $x^2-9x+18<0$?
=== algebra-2
Find the point $p$ in the first quadrant on the line $y=2x$ such that the distance between $p$ and $p'$, the point reflected across the line $y=x$, is equal to $\sqrt{32}$.
=== algebra-3
There are several pairs of integers $(a,b)$ satisfying $a^2-4a+b^2-8b=30$. Find the sum of the sum of the coordinates of all such points.
=== algebra-4
Two real numbers $x$ and $y$ are both chosen at random from the closed interval $[-10,10]$. Find the probability that $x^2+y^2<10$. Express your answer as a common fraction in terms of $\pi$.
=== algebra-5
Find the sum of all real solutions to $(x^2-10x-12)^{x^2+5x+2}=1$
=== algebra-6
Find the maximum value of $\frac{x}{y}$ if $x$ and $y$ are real numbers such that $x^2+y^2-8x-6y+20=0$.
=== algebra-7
Let $r_1,r_2,r_3$ be the (possibly complex) roots of the polynomial $x^3+ax^2+bx+\frac{4}{3}$. How many pairs of integers $a,b$ exist such that $r_1^3+r_2^3+r_3^3=0$ ?
=== algebra-8
A biased coin has a $\frac{6+2\sqrt{3}}{12}$ chance of landing heads, and a $\frac{6-2\sqrt{3}}{12}$ chance of landing tails. What is the probability that the number of times the coin lands heads after being flipped $100$ times is a multiple of $4$? The answer can be expressed as $\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1+a^b}{c\cdot d^e}$ where $a,b,c,d,e$ are positive integers. Find the minimal possible value of $a+b+c+d+e$.
=== algebra-9
Let $a_n$ be the product of the complex roots of $x^{2n}=1$ that are in the first quadrant of the complex plane. That is, roots of the form $a+bi$ where $a,b>0$. Let $r=a_1\cdot a_2\cdot\ldots\cdot a_{10}$. Find the smallest integer $k$ such that $r$ is a root of $x^k=1$.
=== algebra-10
Find the number of ordered integer triplets $x,y,z$ with absolute value less than or equal to $100$ such that $2x^2+3y^2+3z^2+2xy+2xz-4yz<5$
=== discrete-math-1
A fair coin is repeatedly flipped until $2019$ consecutive coin flips are the same. Compute the probability that the first and last flips of the coin come up differently.
=== discrete-math-2
Sylvia has a bag of $10$ coins. Nine are fair coins, but the tenth has tails on both sides. Sylvia draws a coin at random from the bag and flips it without looking. If the coin comes up tails, what is the probability that the coin she drew was the $2$-tailed coin?
=== discrete-math-3
There are $15$ people at a party; each person has $10$ friends. To greet each other each person hugs all their friends. How many hugs are exchanged at this party?
=== discrete-math-4
There exists one pair of positive integers $a,b$ such that $100>a>b>0$ and $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}=\frac{2}{35}$. Find $a+b$.
=== discrete-math-5
Let $2^{1110}\equiv n\pmod{1111}$ with $0\le n<1111$. Compute $n$.
=== discrete-math-6
Define $f(n)=\frac{n^2+n}{2}$. Compute the number of positive integers $n$ such that $f(n)\le 1000$ and $f(n)$ is the product of two prime numbers.
=== discrete-math-7
Call the number of times that the digits of a number change from increasing to decreasing, or vice versa, from the left to right while ignoring consecutive digits that are equal the flux of the number. For example, the flux of $123$ is $0$ (since the digits are always increasing from left to right) and the flux of $12333332$ is $1$, while the flux of $9182736450$ is $8$. What is the average value of the flux of the positive integers from $1$ to $999$, inclusive?
=== discrete-math-8
For a positive integer $n$, define $\phi(n)$ as the number of positive integers less than or equal to $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$. Find the sum of all positive integers $n$ such that $\phi(n)=20$.
=== discrete-math-9
Let $z=\frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{2}+i\sqrt{2}\right)$. The sum
$$\sum_{k=0}^{13}\frac{1}{1-ze^{k\cdot i\pi/7}}$$
can be written in the form $a-bi$. Find $a+b$.
=== discrete-math-10
Let $S(n)$ be the sum of the squares of the positive integers less than and coprime to $n$. For example, $S(5)=1^2+2^2+3^2+4^2$, but $S(4)=1^2+3^2$.

Let $p=2^7-1=127$ and $q=2^5-1=31$ be primes. The quantity $S(pq)$ can be written in the form
$$\frac{p^2q^2}{6}\left(a-\frac{b}{c}\right)$$
where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers, with $b$ and $c$ coprime and $b<c$. Find $a$.

=== geometry-6
Let $ABE$ be a triangle with $AB/3=BE/4=EA/5$. Let $D\ne A$ be on line $AE$ such that $AE=ED$ and $D$ is closer to $E$ than to $A$. Moreover, let $C$ be a point such that $BCDE$ is a parallelogram. Furthermore, let $M$ be on line $CD$ such that $AM$ bisects $\angle BAE$, and let $P$ be the intersection of $AM$ and $BE$. Compute the ratio of $PM$ to the perimeter of $ABE$.
=== geometry-7
Points $ABCD$ are vertices of an isosceles trapezoid, with $AB$ parallel to $CD$, $AB=1$, $CD=2$, and $BC=1$. Point $E$ is chosen uniformly and at random on $CD$, and let point $F$ be the point on $CD$ such that $EC=FD$. Let $G$ denote the intersection of $AE$ and $BF$, not necessarily in the trapezoid. What is the probability that $\angle AGB>30^\circ$?
=== geometry-8
Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB=13$, $BC=14$, and $CA=15$. Let $G$ denote the centroid of $ABC$, and let $G_A$ denote the image of $G$ under a reflection across $BC$, with $G_B$ the image of $G$ under a reflection across $AC$, and $G_C$ the image of $G$ under a reflection across $AB$. Let $O_G$ be the circumcenter of $G_AG_BG_C$ and let $X$ be the intersection of $AO_G$ with $BC$ and $Y$ denote the intersections of $AG$ with $BC$. Compute $XY$.
=== geometry-9
Let $ABCD$ be a tetrahedron with $\angle ABC=\angle ABD=\angle CBD=90^\circ$ and $AB=BC$. Let $E,F,G$ be points on $AD,BD$, and $CD$, respectively, such that each of the quadrilaterals $AEFB$, $BFGC$, and $CGEA$ have an inscribed circle. Let $r$ be the smallest real number such that $\text{area}(EFG)/\text{area}(ABC)\le r$ for all such configurations $A,B,C,D,E,F,G$. If $r$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{\sqrt{a-b\sqrt{c}}}{d}$ where $a,b,c,d$ are positive integers with $\gcd(a,b)$ squarefree and $c$ squarefree, find $a+b+c+d$.
=== geometry-10
