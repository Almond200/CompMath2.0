=== 50
The number $1729$ is the Hardy-Ramanujan number, i.e., the smallest integer which is the sum of two cubes in two different ways ($1729=1^3+12^3=9^3+10^3$). This allows us to write $2017$ as a sum of five different positive cubes (of natural numbers) in two different ways:
$$2017=1^3+12^3+a^3+b^3+c^3=9^3+10^3+a^3+b^3+c^3.$$
If $a<b<c$, find $(a-b+c)/2$.

(A) $0$  (B) $1$  (C) $2$  (D) $3$  (E) $4$
