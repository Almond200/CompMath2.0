=== individual-1
Compute the greatest prime factor of $N$, where
$$N=2018\cdot 517+517\cdot 2812+2812\cdot 666+666\cdot 2018.$$
=== individual-2
Compute the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(a,b)$ that satisfy
$$a^2b^3=20^{18}.$$
=== individual-3
Compute the number of positive three-digit multiples of $3$ whose digits are distinct and nonzero.
=== individual-4
In $\triangle ABC$, $\cos(A)=\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\cos(B)=\dfrac{2}{7}$. Given that the perimeter of $\triangle ABC$ is $24$, compute the area of $\triangle ABC$.
=== individual-5
Compute the product of all positive values of $x$ that satisfy
$$\lfloor x+1\rfloor^{2x}-19\lfloor x+1\rfloor^{x}+48=0.$$
=== individual-6
Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in circle $\omega$. The line containing the median from $A$ meets $\omega$ again at $M$, the line containing the angle bisector of $\angle B$ meets $\omega$ again at $R$, and the line containing the altitude from $C$ meets $\omega$ again at $L$. Given that quadrilateral $ARML$ is a rectangle, compute the degree measure of $\angle BAC$.
=== individual-7
Let $S$ be the set of points $(x,y)$ whose coordinates satisfy $x^2+y^2\le 36$ and $(\max\{x,y\})^2\le 27$. Compute the perimeter of $S$.
=== individual-8
Rectangle $PQRS$ is drawn inside square $ABCD$, as shown. Given that $[APS]=20$ and $[CQR]=18$, compute $[ABCD]$.

=== individual-9
Compute the least positive four-digit integer $N$ for which $N$ and $N+2018$ contain a total of $8$ distinct digits.
=== individual-10
Compute the least positive value of $t$ such that
$$\operatorname{Arcsin}(\sin(\alpha)),\ \operatorname{Arcsin}(\sin(2\alpha)),\ \operatorname{Arcsin}(\sin(7\alpha)),\ \operatorname{Arcsin}(\sin(t\alpha))$$
is a geometric progression for some $\alpha$ with $0<\alpha<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
