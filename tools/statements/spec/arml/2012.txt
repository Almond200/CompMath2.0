=== individual-1
Compute the largest prime divisor of $15!-13!$.
=== individual-2
Three non-overlapping squares of positive integer side lengths each have one vertex at the origin and sides parallel to the coordinate axes. Together, the three squares enclose a region whose area is $41$. Compute the largest possible perimeter of the region.
=== individual-3
A circle with center $O$ and radius $1$ contains chord $\overline{AB}$ of length $1$, and point $M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{AB}$. If the perpendicular to $\overline{AO}$ through $M$ intersects $\overline{AO}$ at $P$, compute $[MAP]$.
=== individual-4
Suppose that $p$ and $q$ are two-digit prime numbers such that $p^2-q^2=2p+6q+8$. Compute the largest possible value of $p+q$.
=== individual-5
The four zeros of the polynomial $x^4+jx^2+kx+225$ are distinct real numbers in arithmetic progression. Compute the value of $j$.
=== individual-6
Compute the smallest positive integer $n$ such that
$$n,\ \lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor,\ \lfloor\sqrt[3]{n}\rfloor,\ \lfloor\sqrt[4]{n}\rfloor,\ \lfloor\sqrt[5]{n}\rfloor,\ \lfloor\sqrt[6]{n}\rfloor,\ \lfloor\sqrt[7]{n}\rfloor,\ \text{and}\ \lfloor\sqrt[8]{n}\rfloor$$
are distinct.
=== individual-7
If $n$ is a positive integer, then $n!!$ is defined to be $n(n-2)(n-4)\cdots 2$ if $n$ is even and $n(n-2)(n-4)\cdots 1$ if $n$ is odd. For example, $8!!=8\cdot 6\cdot 4\cdot 2=384$ and $9!!=9\cdot 7\cdot 5\cdot 3\cdot 1=945$. Compute the number of positive integers $n$ such that $n!!$ divides $2012!!$.
=== individual-8
On the complex plane, the parallelogram formed by the points $0$, $z$, $\frac{1}{z}$, and $z+\frac{1}{z}$ has area $\frac{35}{37}$, and the real part of $z$ is positive. If $d$ is the smallest possible value of $\left|z+\frac{1}{z}\right|$, compute $d^2$.
=== individual-9
One face of a $2\times 2\times 2$ cube is painted (not the entire cube), and the cube is cut into eight $1\times 1\times 1$ cubes. The small cubes are reassembled randomly into a $2\times 2\times 2$ cube. Compute the probability that no paint is showing.
=== individual-10
In triangle $ABC$, $AB=BC$. A trisector of $\angle B$ intersects $\overline{AC}$ at $D$. If $AB$, $AC$, and $BD$ are integers and $AB-BD=7$, compute $AC$.
=== local-individual-1
Compute the number of positive integers $k$ such that $5^4\le k^2\le 4^5$.
=== local-individual-2
In rectangle $ABCD$, points $R$ and $S$ trisect side $\overline{AB}$ ($AR=RS=SB$). If $CS=2\sqrt{13}$ and $CR=10$, compute the area of $ABCD$.
=== local-individual-3
A fair $6$-sided die is rolled four times. Compute the probability that the numbers rolled were rolled in a strictly increasing order (e.g., 1-2-3-5, not 1-2-2-4 or 1-2-4-3).
=== local-individual-4
Compute the side length of the smallest equilateral triangle whose vertices all lie on the $x$- or $y$- axes and that contains the point $(20,12)$ on its boundary.
=== local-individual-5
Compute the value of the infinite sum:
$$\frac{1}{1\times 3}+\frac{1}{2\times 4}+\frac{1}{3\times 5}+\frac{1}{4\times 6}+\frac{1}{5\times 7}+\cdots.$$
=== local-individual-6
In the convex quadrilateral $ARML$, $AR=AL$, $RM=ML$, and $\text{m}\angle M=2\text{m}\angle A$. If the area of $ARL$ is $2012$ times the area of $MRL$, compute $\cos\angle A$.
=== local-individual-7
Compute the volume of the region $R=\{(x,y,z):0\le x,y\le 4,\ 0\le z\le\lfloor x+y\rfloor\}$, where $\lfloor w\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $w$.
=== local-individual-8
Compute the solution $(x,y,z)$ to the following system of equations, where $x$, $y$, and $z$ are all positive integers:
$$\begin{aligned}x+xy+xyz&=564\\ y+xz+xyz&=354.\end{aligned}$$
=== local-individual-9
Compute the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $77n$ is the product of three consecutive integers.
=== local-individual-10
Pentagon $ABCDE$ is inscribed in a circle. $AB=12$, $BC=32$, $CD=8$, and diagonal $\overline{BD}$ bisects diagonal $\overline{AC}$. Compute the number of possible integer values of $AE$.
