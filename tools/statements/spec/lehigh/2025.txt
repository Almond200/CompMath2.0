=== 1
Points $A$ and $B$ are connected by a $10$-mile trail. There is a water fountain on the trail at point $C$. A group of $8$ hikers walked from point $A$ to $C$, while a group of $12$ hikers walked from $B$ to $C$. The sum of the distances traveled by all hikers in the group that started at $A$ was equal to the sum of the distances traveled by all hikers in the group that started at $B$. How many miles was the distance from $A$ to $C$?
=== 2
List all solutions of $x^2+|x|-6=0$.
=== 3
An isosceles right triangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$. There are three regions inside the circle but outside the triangle. Two of them are equal. What is the area of one of these equal areas?
=== 4
How many arrangements of the letters of CYCLIC have the property that the C's are evenly spaced; i.e., the number of letters between the first two C's equals the number between the last two?
=== 5
If $a$, $b$, $c$, and $d$ are four distinct integers satisfying $\min(a,b)=3$, $\min(b,c)=0$, $\max(a,c)=3$, and $\max(c,d)=6$, what is the fourth smallest possible value of $a+b+c+d$?
=== 6
If $f(x)$ is a polynomial satisfying $2f(x)+f(1-x)=x^3$, what is the sum of the coefficients of $f(x)$?
=== 7
Consider $5$ points in the plane, no three of which are collinear. Let $n$ be the number of circles that can be drawn through at least three of the points. What are the possible values of $n$?
=== 8
Simplify $\dfrac{2041^3-2025^3-16^3}{2041\cdot 2025}$.
=== 9
If $\sin x+\sin^2x+\sin^3x+\cdots=4$, list all possible values of $\cos x+\cos^2x+\cos^3x+\cdots$.
=== 10
Six vertex points are evenly spaced around a circle of radius $2$, and six line segments connecting pairs of them are drawn as indicated in Figure 1.1. What is the area inside the $12$-sided polygon bounded by bold lines in the figure?

=== 11
Let $f(n)=\displaystyle\sum_{d=1}^{n}\left\lfloor\frac nd\right\rfloor$ and $g(n)=f(n)-f(n-1)$. For how many values of $n$ from $1$ to $100$ is $g(n)$ even?
=== 12
What is the area of the set of points in the plane satisfying $|x|+|y|+|x+y|\le 1000$?
=== 13
A bug moves on the surface of a cube of side length $2$. It is attached by a leash of length $2$ to a pole at the center of one face of the cube. What is the area of the region on the cube which the bug can access?
=== 14
Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{100}\left\lfloor i^{3/2}\right\rfloor+\sum_{i=0}^{1000}\left\lfloor i^{2/3}\right\rfloor$.
=== 15
Alice and Bob have identical decks with $3$ red cards, $3$ white cards, and $3$ blue cards. They take turns choosing a random card from their deck, without replacement. Alice goes first. What is the probability that Alice chooses all her red cards before Bob has chosen any red cards?
=== 16
Circles of positive integer radius $a$, $b$, and $c$ with $c<b<a$ are mutually tangent, and are tangent to the same line at distinct points. Find $(a,b,c)$ for which $c$ is as small as possible, and $a$ and $b$ as small as possible for this $c$.
=== 17
Compute the nonzero real triple $(a,b,c)$ such that
$$\left(x^2+2ax+b\right)^2+2a\left(x^2+2ax+b\right)-b=(x-c)^4$$
is an identity of polynomials.
=== 18
List all positive integers $n$ such that the sum of all positive integers that are $\le n$ and relatively prime to $n$ equals $2n$.
=== 19
What is the $500$th smallest positive integer whose base-$3$ expansion has no consecutive equal digits? For example, the fourth smallest such number is $5$, since the base-$3$ expansions of the first five positive integers are $1$, $2$, $10$, $11$, and $12$.
=== 20
Given that $641$ is a prime divisor of $2^{32}+1$, find the smallest positive integer $k$ such that $\left(2^{16}+1\right)^k\equiv 1\pmod{641}$.
=== 21
Triangle $ABC$ has sides of length $5$, $6$, and $7$. Point $P$ lies inside the triangle with angles $PAB$, $PBC$, and $PCA$ each equal to $\alpha$. Find $\tan\alpha$.
=== 22
An integer randomly selected from the interval $[1,5]$ is written on the board. At each step, the number $x$ on the board is replaced by an integer chosen randomly from the interval
$$\begin{cases}[1,x-1]&\text{if }x>1\\ [1,5]&\text{if }x=1.\end{cases}$$
Let $P_n(x)$ denote the probability that the number on the board after $n$ steps is $x$. Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}P_n(2)$.
=== 23
Let $ABC$ be a triangle with circumcenter $O$ and $\angle B$ obtuse. Suppose the tangent to its circumcircle at $A$ intersects ray $CB$ at $D$, and that $AO=AD=2$. Suppose that $F$ lies on $BC$ with $AF\perp OD$, and let $E$ be the point where $AF$ meets $OD$. Let $H$ be the point where the circle passing through $ADO$ meets $BC$. What is the area of triangle $OEF$ if $FH=\frac{\sqrt3}{3}$?
=== 24
Four distinct points are selected randomly from inside a circle. These determine four triangles (including the possibility of degenerate triangles). Then two of the four triangles are randomly selected. What is the probability that the center of the circle lies in the union of the interiors of the two triangles selected?
=== 25
List all positive integers $n$ for which there is a convex $n$-gon whose angles, in degrees, are distinct integers in arithmetic progression. List them in order in concatenated form; i.e., not separated by commas. For example, the correct answer begins $345$.
