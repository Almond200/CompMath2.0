=== 1
How many right triangles have all side lengths integers and both legs of length a prime number?
=== 2
Find a number $k$ satisfying
$$\sqrt[3]{27\sqrt3-34\sqrt2}=\sqrt3+k\sqrt2.$$
=== 3
Evaluate the continued fraction $6+\cfrac{1}{3+\cfrac{1}{6+\cfrac{1}{3+\cdots}}}$.
=== 4
How many ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers satisfy
$$a^2+b^2=ab+7?$$
=== 5
Let $x$ be a real number for which $x^2-3x+1=0$. What is the value of $x^3+\dfrac1{x^3}$?
=== 6
Andy and Beth cross a lake in a straight path with the help of a one-seat kayak. Each can paddle at $7$ km/hr and swim at $3$ km/hr. They start from the same point at the same time, with Andy paddling and Beth swimming. After a while, Andy stops the kayak and starts swimming. The kayak doesn't move until Beth gets to it. Then she starts paddling the kayak. They arrive at the far side of the lake at the same time, $90$ minutes after they began. For how many minutes was the kayak not being paddled?
=== 7
Dawn has $P$ packages, each containing $19$ candies. If she divides all the candies equally among $7$ friends, there are $4$ candies left over. If she divides them equally among $11$ friends there is $1$ candy left over. What is the smallest possible value of $P$?
=== 8
A bridge hand consists of $13$ cards, randomly selected from a standard $52$-card deck. When evaluating a hand, each Ace counts $4$ points, each King counts as $3$ points, each Queen $2$ points, and each Jack $1$ point. Given that a hand has exactly four cards that are Ace, King, Queen, or Jack, what is the probability that it has exactly $13$ points?
=== 9
Write as a reduced fraction
$$\frac{1}{1\cdot 2\cdot 3}+\frac{1}{2\cdot 3\cdot 4}+\frac{1}{3\cdot 4\cdot 5}+\cdots+\frac{1}{98\cdot 99\cdot 100}.$$
=== 10
In a class of $100$ students, $90$ are studying Spanish, $80$ are studying French, $70$ are studying German, and $60$ are studying Italian. No student is studying all four languages. What is the number of students who are studying both Spanish and French?
=== 11
The Gaussian integers are complex numbers whose real and imaginary parts are both integers. The solutions of
$$x^4+8x^3+14x^2-8x+145=0$$
are Gaussian integers. What is the largest value of $a$ such that $a+bi$ is a solution of this equation for some $b$?
=== 12
Find the smallest possible value of the expression $\left\lfloor\dfrac{a+b}{c}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{b+c}{a}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{c+a}{b}\right\rfloor$ where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers.
=== 13
Let $f(k)=\lfloor k/3\rfloor$. How many positive integers $n<1000$ have the property that none of $n$, $f(n)$, $f(f(n))$, and $f(f(f(n)))$ are divisible by $3$?
=== 14
Triangle $ABC$ has perimeter $25$. Points $A'$ and $B'$ lie on sides $AC$ and $BC$, respectively, such that $A'B'$ is tangent to a circle inscribed inside the triangle and is parallel to $AB$. If $A'B'$ has length $2$, what is the largest possible length of $AB$?
=== 15
Let $r_1$, $r_2$, $r_3$, and $r_4$ be the roots of the polynomial $P(x)=9x^4-3x^3-101x^2+195x-100$. Compute
$$(r_1+r_2+r_3)(r_1+r_2+r_4)(r_1+r_3+r_4)(r_2+r_3+r_4).$$
=== 16
Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n}{n^4+4}$.
=== 17
Let $ABC$ be an isosceles triangle with $AB=AC$, and let $\ell$ be the line which bisects $\angle ABC$. The line through $C$ parallel to $\ell$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at $D$. Let $P$ be the intersection of lines $AD$ and $\ell$, and suppose that $AP:PD=2:3$. Find the value of $\cos(\angle BAC)$.
=== 18
The number $989\cdot 1001\cdot 1007+320$ is a product of three primes. Write them, separated by commas, without parentheses?
=== 19
How many $10$-digit numbers can be formed using just the digits $1$, $2$, or $3$ such that the number of occurrences of the digit $1$ is even?
=== 20
Let $\dfrac{1}{1-x-x^2-x^3}=\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nx^n$. What is the sum of all positive integers $n$ for which $a_{n-1}=n^2$?
=== 21
Let $x$ and $y$ be positive integers such that $\dfrac{xy}{x+y}>2$. What is the minimum possible value of $\dfrac{xy}{x+y}$?
=== 22
Define a sequence by $a_1=2021$ and, for $n\ge 1$,
$$a_{n+1}=\begin{cases}a_n/2&\text{if }a_n\text{ is even}\\ a_n-1&\text{if }a_n\text{ is odd.}\end{cases}$$
What is the smallest value of $n$ for which $a_n=0$?
=== 23
Tommy rolls a fair $6$-sided die until a $6$ occurs for the first time. If $n$ is the number of rolls preceding the first $6$, he wins $n^2$ dollars. On average, how many dollars does he win?
=== 24
Beginning at a vertex, an ant moves from vertex to adjacent vertex on a regular octahedron, with all moves equally likely. What is the probability that, after $10$ moves, it is back where it started?
=== 25
Let $\phi(n)$ denote the number of positive integers less than or equal to $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$. For how many positive integers $n$ less than $2021$ is $n$ divisible by $\phi(n)$?
=== 26
What is the minimum value of
$$|\sin x+\cos x+\tan x+\cot x+\sec x+\csc x|$$
for all $x$ for which the expression is defined?
=== 27
Cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ has $AB=4$, $BC=1$, $CD=2$, and $DA=3$. The midpoints of $BC$ and $DA$ are $P$ and $Q$, respectively. Find $PQ^2$. (Note: this is length squared.)
=== 28
What is the number of ordered pairs of integers, with $1\le m,n\le 1000$ such that $m/(n+1)<\sqrt2<(m+1)/n$? Recall $\sqrt2\approx 1.4142\cdots$.
=== 29
The integers from $1$ to $12$ are written in some order. A "move" consists of interchanging an even number with an odd number in the list. What is the smallest number $n$ such that, for any initial order, the integers can always be brought to their usual order $1,2,\dots,12$ in $n$ moves?
=== 30
Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$. For each side of the triangle, a circle is drawn outside the triangle but inside the circle, tangent to the side of the triangle at its midpoint and tangent to the circle. The radii of two of the circles are $\frac23$ and $\frac2{11}$. What is the radius of the third circle?
