=== 1
How many positive integers are divisors of $2024$?
=== 2
What is the smallest positive integer $x$ such that $2024-x$ is the cube of an integer?
=== 3
A square with sides of length $4$ is partitioned into four congruent subsquares. A circle passes through the centers of the four subsquares. What is the area of the circle?
=== 4
In a certain football league the only possible scores are $7$ for a touchdown and $3$ for a field goal. Nothing else is possible, so it is not like ordinary football. How many positive scores are not possible?
=== 5
You flip a coin five times and heads occurs three times. Given this information, what is the probability that the first two flips were heads?
=== 6
Find all ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ which satisfy
$$a^2+2b^2-2bc=100\quad\text{and}\quad 2ab-c^2=100.$$
=== 7
Suppose $a>b>0$ and $\dfrac ab+\dfrac ba=6$. Evaluate $\left(\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}\right)^3$.
=== 8
Let $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$ denote the fractional part of the real number $x$. List all real numbers $x$ such that $\lfloor x\rfloor^2=x\{x\}+\{x\}^2$.
=== 9
Let $f(x)=x^2-2x+t$. Find all real numbers $t$ such that for all $a,b,c\in[0,3]$ (not necessarily distinct) there exists a triangle with positive area with side lengths $f(a)$, $f(b)$, and $f(c)$.
=== 10
Let $f(x)=\frac12+\log_{10}\left(\frac{x}{1-x}\right)$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{99}f\left(\frac{i}{100}\right)$?
=== 11
A circle inscribed in triangle $ABC$ touches $AB$ at $D$ with $AD=5$ and $DB=3$. Find the length of $BC$ if angle $A$ is $60$ degrees.
=== 12
Find all real numbers $t$ such that there are exactly five integers $n$ satisfying the inequalities below. (You may express your answer using interval notation or inequalities.)
$$\frac{2n+5}{3}-n>-5,\qquad\frac{n+3}{2}-t<n.$$
=== 13
How many ordered pairs of integers $(m,n)$ satisfy the equation $\frac1m+\frac1n=\frac12$? Note that negative integers are allowed, as well as positive.
=== 14
An ant is moving around the vertices of a cube. Each move takes it from its current vertex to one of the three adjacent vertices. What is the probability that after four moves it is back where it started?
=== 15
Suppose functions $f$ and $g$ satisfy that for all real numbers $x$ and $y$, $f(x+g(y))=2x+y+5$. Find an explicit expression for $g(x+f(y))$.
=== 16
A regular $5$-pointed star (equal angles at each tip and equal lines connecting tips) has total length of the $5$ lines connecting the tips equal to $1$. What is the perimeter of the regular pentagon in its interior? Your answer should involve a specific trig function and specific angle, expressed in degrees.

=== 17
There is a pot of candies and a group of people who want them. Each person gets only one chance and must take exactly $1/3$ or $1/2$ of the remaining candy. What is the largest number of people who could take candies from some initial amount less than $1000$? Fractional candies are not allowed. The initial amount is chosen to accommodate the maximum number of people.
=== 18
Find all values of $k$ such that the equation
$$4x^2+4(2-k)x-k^2=0$$
has two real solutions $x_1$ and $x_2$ satisfying $|x_1|=2+|x_2|$.
=== 19
If $n$ is a positive integer, let $p(n)$ be the product of the digits of $n$, and let $r(n)$ be the integer obtained by reversing the order of the digits of $n$. For example, $r(125)=521$. Find all positive integers $n$ such that $p(n)>0$ and $n\cdot r(n)=1000+p(n)$.
=== 20
Let $x_{400}$ be $400$th smallest positive solution of the equation $x-\frac\pi2=\tan x$. Find the greatest integer that does not exceed $\frac12x_{400}$.
=== 21
Find the minimum value of $(3\cos\alpha+5\sin\beta-8)^2+(3\sin\alpha+5\cos\beta-15)^2$ for all real numbers $\alpha$ and $\beta$.
=== 22
Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB=9$, $BC=10$, and $CA=17$. Let $B'$ be the reflection of the point $B$ across the line $AC$. Let $G$ (resp. $G'$) be the centroid of triangle $ABC$ (resp. $AB'C$). What is the length of $GG'$?
=== 23
Suppose $a>1$ and $x,y>0$ satisfy
$$(\log_ax)^2+(\log_ay)^2-\log_a\left(x^2y^2\right)\le 2$$
and $\log_ay\ge 1$. Find the interval of values of $\log_a\left(x^2y\right)$.
=== 24
Find the number of integers $n$ such that $n$ is a multiple of $5$, and the base-$11$ expansion of $n$, $\overline{def}_{11}$, satisfies $0<d<f$.
=== 25
Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=4$, $BC=5$, and $CA=6$. The circumcircle of $ABC$ is also the incircle of triangle $A'B'C'$, with $A$ on $B'C'$, $B$ on $A'C'$, and $C$ on $A'B'$. What is the length of $B'C'$?
=== 26
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are inverse functions. Let $F(x)=f(x+1)-2$ and $G(x)=g(2x+1)$, and suppose that $F$ and $G$ are also inverse functions. Finally, suppose $f(1)=4$. Find $f(100)$.
=== 27
$27$ balls, numbered $1$ through $27$, are placed in three bowls labeled A, B, and C with at least four balls in each bowl. If the averages of the numbers on the balls in bowls A, B, and C are $15$, $3$, and $18$, respectively, what is the largest possible number on the ball with the smallest number in bowl A?
=== 28
Let $r_1,\dots,r_7$ denote the distinct roots of the polynomial $x^7-5$. What is the value of $\displaystyle\prod_{1\le i<j\le 7}\left(r_i+r_j\right)^2$?
=== 29
Find all $5$-digit numbers $\overline{abcde}$, not containing the digit $0$, such that $e\mid\overline{de}\mid\overline{cde}\mid\overline{bcde}\mid\overline{abcde}$. That is, repeatedly removing the left digit leaves a number which is a divisor of the previous number. The digits need not be distinct.
=== 30
Cards numbered $1$ to $20$ are placed randomly in slots numbered $1$ to $20$, with one card in each slot. A transposition consists of interchanging the slots of two cards whose numbers have the opposite parity, leaving other cards fixed. How many transpositions are required to get every card into the slot with the same number from any initial arrangement?
