=== 1
Express $\frac13+\frac25$ as a reduced fraction.
=== 2
List all values of $x$ that satisfy $\dfrac{(x+2)(2x-1)}{x-3}=0$.
=== 3
What is the area of a triangle whose sides are $13$, $13$, and $10$?
=== 4
How many of the $2$-digit numbers from $10$ to $99$ have the property that both digits are perfect squares? For example, $10$ is the smallest such number and $99$ is the largest.
=== 5
How many digits are in the base $10$ number $4^{22}\cdot 5^{40}$? For example, $9001$ has four digits.
=== 6
Find $A$ if there is a polynomial identity
$$\left(x^3+2x^2-3x-2\right)\left(x^4+3x^3+Ax^2-6x+1\right)=x^7+5x^6+5x^5-13x^4-23x^3+16x^2+9x-2.$$
=== 7
If $1.0000042376^2=1.00000xyz521795725376$, what is the value of $x+y+z$?
=== 8
The ratio of children to adults at a party is $2{:}3$. A busload of $30$ more children arrives at the party, and now the ratio of children to adults is $3{:}2$. How many people were at the party before the bus arrived?
=== 9
Find the area of the region consisting of all points $(x,y)$ satisfying $1\le|x|+|y|\le 2$.
=== 10
A sequence is defined by $a_1=1$ and, for $n\ge 1$,
$$a_{n+1}=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }a_n=0\text{ and }n\text{ is odd}\\2&\text{if }a_n=0\text{ and }n\text{ is even}\\1&\text{if }a_n=1\text{ and }n\text{ is odd}\\0&\text{if }a_n=1\text{ and }n\text{ is even}\\1&\text{if }a_n=2.\end{cases}$$
How many of the numbers $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_{2012}$ are equal to $2$?
=== 11
You are trying to guess an integer between $1$ and $1000$, inclusive. Each time you make a guess, which must be an integer, you are told whether your number is too high, too low, or correct. What is the smallest number of guesses required to guarantee that you will have guessed the number?
=== 12
A number system based on $26$ uses the letters of the alphabet as its digits, with $A=0$, $B=1$, $C=2,\dots,Y=24$, and $Z=25$. Express $ONE+ONE$ in this system.
=== 13
A circle is inscribed in quadrilateral $ABCD$. If $AB=4$, $BC=5$, and $CD=8$, what is $DA$?
=== 14
What is the $4$-tuple $(w,x,y,z)$ which satisfies all four of the following equations?
$$\begin{aligned}w+x+y&=4\\x+y+z&=-5\\y+z+w&=0\\z+w+x&=-8\end{aligned}$$
=== 15
The outside of a cube is painted black, and then it is cut up into $64$ smaller congruent cubes. How many of the smaller cubes have at least one face painted black?
=== 16
This problem deals with $5$-digit numbers, and for the purposes of this problem, we allow $0$'s as the initial digits. Thus the number of $5$-digit numbers is $10^5$ under this interpretation (which differs from the usual convention). Write as a decimal the probability that a randomly selected $5$-digit number contains exactly $4$ distinct digits (under the interpretation of this problem).
=== 17
How many ordered pairs $(x,y)$ of integers satisfy $x^2+6x+y^2=16$?
=== 18
Circles of radius $10$ and $17$ intersect at two points. The segment connecting these points of intersection has length $16$. List all possible values for the distance between the centers of the circles?
=== 19
Of all the nonempty subsets $S$ of $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}$, how many do not contain the number $|S|$, where $|S|$ denotes the number of elements in $S$? For example, $\{3,4\}$ is one such subset, since it does not contain the number $2$.
=== 20
Let $(x_1,y_1),\dots,(x_6,y_6)$ denote the vertices of a regular hexagon whose center is at $(2,0)$ and which has one vertex at $(3,0)$. Let $z_1=x_1+iy_1,\dots,z_6=x_6+iy_6$ denote the corresponding complex numbers. What is the product $z_1\cdots z_6$ of these six complex numbers?
=== 21
What is the value of the continued fraction
$$3+\cfrac{1}{4+\cfrac{1}{3+\cfrac{1}{4+\cdots}}}\ ?$$
The $3$'s and $4$'s alternate indefinitely.
=== 22
List all ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ of positive integers which satisfy $a+\frac bc=11$ and $b+\frac ac=14$.
=== 23
Let $F_n$ denote the Fibonacci numbers, defined by $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$ for $n\ge 1$. Express the infinite sum
$$S=\frac{F_1}{5}+\frac{F_2}{5^2}+\frac{F_3}{5^3}+\frac{F_4}{5^4}+\cdots$$
as a reduced fraction.
=== 24
Let $A_1,\dots,A_6$ denote the vertices of a regular hexagon inscribed in the circle $x^2+y^2=3$. Circles of radius $1$ are drawn with centers at each of these six points, and a seventh circle of radius $1$ is drawn, centered at the origin. What is the area of the region common to at least two of the $7$ circles of radius $1$; i.e., the union of the intersections?
=== 25
Let $A=\sin x+\cos x$. Write $\sin^4x+\cos^4x$ as a polynomial in $A$.
=== 26
What is the largest positive integer $n$ such that $n$ is divisible by every positive integer $m$ which satisfies $m^2+4\le n$?
=== 27
Points $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ lie in a plane, with $A$, $B$, and $C$ in order in a straight line. The angles between some of these points, measured in degrees, satisfy $BDC=78$, $DBC>DCB$, $ABD=4x+y$ and $DCB=x+y$, for some numbers $x$ and $y$. How many positive integer values can $y$ take on, satisfying all these conditions?
=== 28
Alice and Bill are walking in opposite directions along the same route between A and B. Alice is going from A to B, and Bill from B to A. They start at the same time. They pass each other $3$ hours later. Alice arrives at B $2.5$ hours before Bill arrives at A. How many hours are required for Bill's trip from B to A?
=== 29
What is the smallest positive integer that can be written as the difference of two positive perfect squares in at least three distinct ways?
=== 30
What is the ratio $V^2/A^3$, where $V$ is the volume of a regular octahedron and $A$ is its surface area?
=== 31
In triangle $ABC$, the median from $A$ is perpendicular to the median from $B$. If $AC=6$ and $BC=7$, then what is $AB$?
=== 32
Eleven points are arranged on a semicircle with five on the straight line segment and six on the arc. See the diagram below for a possible configuration. Every pair of these points is joined by a straight line segment, and it turns out that no three of the line segments intersect at a common point in the interior of the semicircle. How many points are there in the interior of the semicircle where two of the line segments intersect?

=== 33
A trapezoid has ratio of its bases $2{:}1$, ratio of its legs (nonparallel sides) $2{:}1$, and ratio of its diagonals $2{:}1$. What is the ratio of its shorter base to shorter leg?
=== 34
If $x$ and $y$ are randomly selected real numbers between $0$ and $1$, what is the probability that the integer closest to $\frac{y-x}{y+x}$ is odd?
=== 35
In triangle $ABC$, $AB=3$, $BC=4$, and $AC=6$. If $BC$ is extended through $C$ to $D$ so that $CD=BC$, what is $AD$?
=== 36
Alice, Bill, Chris, Don, and Emily take a test. In how many different orders can they finish if ties are allowed? (For example, if there were three people, it would be $13$, since there are $6$ orders without ties, $1$ with a $3$-way tie, $3$ with a $2$-way tie for first, and $3$ with a $2$-way tie for last.)
=== 37
List all positive integers $b$ for which there is a positive integer $a<b$ such that exactly $1/100$ of the consecutive integers $a^2,a^2+1,\dots,b^2$ are perfect squares.
=== 38
Let $a_n$ denote the $n$th smallest positive integer for which the sum of its decimal digits equals $3$. For example, $a_1=3$, $a_2=12$, $a_3=21$, and $a_4=30$. How many digits are there in $a_{2012}$?
=== 39
You flip a fair coin repeatedly until either four consecutive Heads (H) or six consecutive tails (T) occur. What is the probability that the sequence HHHH occurs before the sequence TTTTTT?
=== 40
Eight points on the circumference of a circle are chosen and all $\binom82=28$ chords connecting them are drawn. It turns that no three chords intersect in the same point in the interior of the circle. Into how many regions do these chords divide the interior of the circle?
