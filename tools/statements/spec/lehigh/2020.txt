=== 1
Simplify $1/\left(\frac15-\frac16\right)$.
=== 2
What is the area of a triangle with side lengths $16$, $30$, and $34$?
=== 3
How many liters of $20\%$ vinegar solution should be added to $4$ liters of $50\%$ vinegar solution to make a $30\%$ vinegar solution?
=== 4
Alex can paint a room in $3$ hours. Working together efficiently without overlapping, Alex and Chris can paint it in $72$ minutes. How many minutes would it take Chris to paint it alone?
=== 5
Professor Davis prefers to give the Saturday Lehigh Contest on February $29$ whenever possible. In what year will be the next time (after $2020$) that February $29$ falls on a Saturday?
=== 6
If a glasses-wearing student enters the library, the fraction of students wearing glasses will be $1/4$. If, instead, a glasses-wearing student leaves the library, the fraction wearing glasses will be $1/5$. How many students are in the library (before either hypothetical change)?
=== 7
Suppose $p>0$ and $x^2+px+1=0$ has solutions which differ by $1$. What is the value of $p$?
=== 8
Points $A$ and $B$ lie on a circle of radius $15$ with $AB=24$. Point $C$ lies outside the circle on ray $AB$ with $BC=28$. What is the distance from $C$ to the center of the circle?
=== 9
What is the smallest positive integer for which the product of its digits equals $16{,}000$?
=== 10
What is the minimum value of $x^2+y^2$ for all $(x,y)$ satisfying $(x-5)^2+(y-12)^2=196$?
=== 11
What is the area of a trapezoid $ABCD$ with parallel sides $AD=2$ and $BC=8$, while $AB=6$ and $DC=4$?
=== 12
Compared to $20$ years ago, the number of boys taking this contest increased by $20\%$, the number of girls increased by $30\%$, and the total number of students increased by $22\%$. What fraction of the students taking the contest this year are girls? (Fictitious numbers.)
=== 13
Let $r_1$, $r_2$, $r_3$, and $r_4$ denote the (possibly complex) roots of the polynomial $p(x)=20x^4+13x^3+11x^2+16$. What is the numerical value of $(r_1+1)(r_2+1)(r_3+1)(r_4+1)$?
=== 14
List all positive integers $n$ for which $\lfloor n^2/5\rfloor$ is a prime number.
=== 15
Point $E$ is the midpoint of $AD$ in square $ABCD$. Point $F$ is the intersection of $BE$ and $AC$. What fraction of the area of the square is contained in quadrilateral $CDEF$?
=== 16
What is the sum of the infinite series
$$1+\frac12+\frac13+\frac14+\frac16+\frac18+\frac19+\frac1{12}+\cdots$$
consisting of the reciprocals of all positive integers which have no prime factors greater than $3$?

=== 19
How many integers from $10000$ to $99999$ have distinct digits such that the last digit equals the sum of the first four?
=== 20
Among families with exactly two children, at least one of which is a boy born on Sunday, what fraction have two boys? Assume genders are equally likely, days of birth are equally likely, and these are independent.
=== 21
What is the smallest integer $N$ satisfying $\left(m+\frac1{2021}\right)^2<N<\left(m+\frac1{2020}\right)^2$ for some positive integer $m$?
=== 22
How many positive divisors does $56^4-20^4$ have?
=== 23
If $\log_4(x+2y)+\log_4(x-2y)=1$, what is the smallest possible value of $x-y$?

=== 25
A $3$-by-$3$ grid is filled randomly with the integers from $1$ to $9$, with each of these integers included once. What is the probability that each row sum is odd and each column sum is odd?
=== 26
A point $P$ inside a square $ABCD$ has $AP=5$, $BP=2\sqrt2$, and $CP=3$. What is the area of the square?

=== 28
What is the largest integer with distinct digits which are all odd such that its product with some positive integer has distinct digits which are all even?
=== 29
A tightrope walker stands in the center of a rope of length $16$ meters. Every minute she walks forward one meter with probability $2/3$ and backward with probability $1/3$. What is the probability that she reaches the forward end before getting to the backward end?
=== 30
A function $f$ satisfies $f(n)=n-2$ if $n>2200$, and $f(n)=f(f(n+5))$ if $n\le 2200$. What is $f(2020)$?
=== 31
A triangle has side lengths $3$, $4$, and $5$. Three spheres are tangent to the plane of the triangle, one at each vertex of the triangle, and are tangent to one another. What is the radius of the largest of these three spheres?
=== 32

=== 33
Let $a_n$ denote the $n$th smallest positive integer for which the sum of the digits equals $7$. For example, $a_1=7$, $a_2=16$, and $a_3=25$. If $a_n=1006$, what is the integer $a_{9n}$?
=== 34
The weekday on which a person is born is random. A large number of people start naming, one at a time, the weekday on which they were born, until all seven weekdays have been mentioned. What is the expected value of the number of people who will have named a day when, for the first time, all seven days have been mentioned? Assume that each day is equally likely at each step.
=== 35
Suppose $p(x)$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients and $q(x)=\frac{p(x)}{x(1-x)}$. If, for all $x\ne 0$ or $1$, $q(x)=q\left(\frac1{1-x}\right)$, $p(2)=5$ and $p(3)=5$, then what is the numerical value of $p(4)$?
=== 36
Let $x$ and $y$ be positive real numbers and $\theta$ an angle which is not an integer multiple of $\pi/2$. Suppose
$$\frac{\sin\theta}{x}=\frac{\cos\theta}{y}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{\cos^4\theta}{x^4}+\frac{\sin^4\theta}{y^4}=\frac{7\sin 2\theta}{x^3y+xy^3}.$$
What is the value of $\frac xy+\frac yx$?
=== 37
What are the three largest integers $a$ for which there exist positive integers $b$ and $c$ satisfying $\frac{a}{a+1}=\frac{b}{b+2}+\frac{c}{c+3}$?
=== 38
What is the number of ordered triples $(a_1,a_2,a_3)$ of positive integers such that $a_1+a_2+a_3=2020$, $a_1$ is not divisible by $2$, $a_2$ is not divisible by $3$, and $a_3$ is not divisible by $4$?
=== 39
Let $C_1$ be a circle of radius $1$, and $P_1$ a square circumscribed around $C_1$. For each $n\ge 2$, let $C_n$ be the circle circumscribed around $P_{n-1}$, and let $P_n$ be a regular $2^{n+1}$-gon circumscribed around $C_n$. What is the numerical value of $\lim_{n\to\infty}r_n$, where $r_n$ is the radius of $C_n$?
=== 40
Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=AC=3$ and $BC=1$. Point $D$ lies on $AB$ so that the inradius of triangles $ACD$ and $BCD$ both equal the same number $r$. What is the value of $r$?
