=== 11
What is the smallest positive integer $p$ for which there is a positive integer $n$ satisfying $2^{n+p}\equiv 2^n\pmod{100}$? (This $p$ is the period of the sequence $\langle 2^n\bmod 100\rangle$, which just considers the last two digits of the numbers.)

=== 13

=== 14

=== 15

=== 16

=== 17

=== 18

=== 19

=== 21

=== 22

=== 23

When the car gets back to Alan, Alan gets into the car, the car turns around and drives until it meets Bill. How many miles has Bill traveled when the car meets him? You may assume that no time is required to turn around or to change passengers.
