=== math-meet-individual-4
Dr. Kraines has $27$ unit cubes, each of which has one side painted red while the other five are white. If he assembles his cubes into one $3\times 3\times 3$ cube by placing each unit cube in a random orientation, what is the probability that the entire surface of the cube will be white, with no red faces visible? If the answer is $2^a3^b5^c$ for integers $a,b,c$, find $|a+b+c|$.

=== math-meet-individual-8
Karthik the farmer is trying to protect his crops from a wildfire. Karthik's land is a $5\times 6$ rectangle divided into $30$ smaller square plots. The $5$ plots on the left edge contain fire, the $5$ plots on the right edge contain blueberry trees, and the other $5\times 4$ plots of land contain banana bushes. Fire will repeatedly spread to all squares with bushes or trees that share a side with a square with fire. How many ways can Karthik replace $5$ of his $20$ plots of banana bushes with firebreaks so that fire will not consume any of his prized blueberry trees?
