=== math-meet-individual-1
Trung took five tests this semester. For his first three tests, his average was $60$, and for the fourth test he earned a $50$. What must he have earned on his fifth test if his final average for all five tests was exactly $60$?
=== math-meet-individual-2
Find the number of pairs of integers $(a,b)$ such that $20a+16b=2016-ab$.
=== math-meet-individual-3
Let $f:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}$ be a strictly increasing function with $f(1)=2016$ and $f(2t)=f(t)+t$ for all $t\in\mathbb{N}$. Find $f(2016)$.
=== math-meet-individual-4
Circles of radius $7,7,18$, and $r$ are mutually externally tangent, where $r=\frac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
=== math-meet-individual-5
A point is chosen at random from within the circumcircle of a triangle with angles $45^\circ,75^\circ,60^\circ$. What is the probability that the point is closer to the vertex with an angle of $45^\circ$ than either of the two other vertices?
=== math-meet-individual-6
Find the largest positive integer $a$ less than $100$ such that for some positive integer $b$, $a-b$ is a prime number and $ab$ is a perfect square.
=== math-meet-individual-7
There is a set of $6$ parallel lines and another set of six parallel lines, where these two sets of lines are not parallel with each other. If Blythe adds $6$ more lines, not necessarily parallel with each other, find the maximum number of triangles that could be made.
=== math-meet-individual-8
Triangle $ABC$ has sides $AB=5$, $AC=4$, and $BC=3$. Let $O$ be any arbitrary point inside $ABC$, and $D\in BC$, $E\in AC$, $F\in AB$, such that $OD\perp BC$, $OE\perp AC$, $OF\perp AB$. Find the minimum value of $OD^2+OE^2+OF^2$.
=== math-meet-individual-9
Find the root with the largest real part to $x^4-3x^3+3x+1=0$ over the complex numbers.
=== math-meet-individual-10
Tony has a board with $2$ rows and $4$ columns. Tony will use $8$ numbers from $1$ to $8$ to fill in this board, each number in exactly one entry. Let array $(a_1,..,a_4)$ be the first row of the board and array $(b_1,..,b_4)$ be the second row of the board.

Let $F=\sum_{i=1}^{4}|a_i-b_i|$, calculate the average value of $F$ across all possible ways to fill in.
