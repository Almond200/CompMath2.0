=== 1
Every day, Alex spends $4$ hours on the Internet. Every week, Alex spends $50$ hours sleeping. Each year, Alex goes to school for $180$ days and spends $8$ hours in class each of these days. Let $I$ be the number of hours on the Internet each year. Let $S$ be the number of hours sleeping each year. Let $C$ be the number of hours in class each year. Assume that there are $52$ weeks in a year and that there are $365$ days in a year. Then,

(A) $C>S>I$  (B) $I>S>C$  (C) $S>I>C$  (D) $I>C>S$  (E) $S>C>I$

=== 5

(A) $2$  (B) $2.5$  (C) $4$  (D) $4.5$  (E) $5$
