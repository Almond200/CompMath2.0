=== 4
Gauss, Euler, Noether, and Ramanujan each have a number. The numbers, not necessarily in this order, are $272$, $1729$, $1882$, and $65537$. Euler's number is smaller than Noether's number. Ramanujan's number is $1729$. Gauss's number is the largest. Which is Noether's number?

(A) $272$  (B) $1729$  (C) $1882$  (D) $65537$  (E) It cannot be determined from the given information.
=== 5

What is the number of elements in $A\cup B$?

(A) $100$  (B) $99$  (C) $98$  (D) $97$  (E) $96$
=== 6

(A) $11.2<x<11.4$  (B) $11.4<x<11.6$  (C) $11.6<x<11.8$  (D) $11.8<x<12.0$  (E) $12.0<x<12.2$
=== 7

$$\log_2(8x),\quad\log_4(16x),\quad\log_8(2x)$$

(A) $1$  (B) $2$  (C) $4$  (D) $8$  (E) $16$
=== 8
Snow White has a bag of Halloween candy to distribute to the seven dwarfs. Dopey suggests that she divide the candy equally among the three youngest dwarfs. Snow White says she cannot do that because there would be one piece of candy left over. Doc suggests that she divide the candy among the $5$ oldest dwarfs. Again, Snow White refuses because there would be one piece of candy left over. Instead, she divides the candy evenly among all seven dwarfs, and there are no pieces of candy left over. Let $N$ be the smallest possible number of pieces of candy. Then

(A) $N<20$  (B) $20<N<40$  (C) $40<N<60$  (D) $60<N<80$  (E) $80<N$
=== 9

(A) $2\sqrt5/5$  (B) $\sqrt3/2$  (C) $\sqrt5/2$  (D) $\sqrt6/3$  (E) $\sqrt2/2$

(A) $y<10$  (B) $10<y<15$  (C) $15<y<20$  (D) $20<y<25$  (E) $25<y$
